About

Made in the Cotswolds, with care

Hi, I'm the person behind Cotswolds Glow AI. With a background in creativity — you might know me from Pip and the Bad Seed — and a genuine passion for what AI tools can do for small businesses, I set out to bridge that gap.

I saw too many brilliant local businesses with outdated websites or no online presence at all, losing customers to competitors who were simply more visible. Traditional agencies were either too expensive, too slow, or just didn't feel personal.

So I built something different. Cotswolds Glow AI combines the speed and intelligence of modern AI tools with real human creativity and a genuine understanding of this corner of the world.

Whether you run a café in Chipping Campden, a B&B in Bourton-on-the-Water, or a coaching practice in Cirencester — I'd love to help your business glow.
